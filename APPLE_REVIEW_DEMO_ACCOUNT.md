## Questions Apple Reviewers May Have

### Q: "Why do you need location permission?"
**A:** The core functionality of TheDailyPoop is a social map where users see where their friends have dropped. Location is essential to place drops on the map.

### Q: "Why do you collect date of birth and gender?"
**A:** These are **optional** fields. Users can skip them entirely. They're only used for optional personalization and badges (e.g., "30+ Club" badge).

### Q: "How do ads work?"
**A:** We use Google AdMob with native ads (in feed), interstitial ads (after drops), and banner ads (on map). Test ads show during review; real ads serve after AdMob approval.

### Q: "Is Sign in with Apple the only auth method?"
**A:** Yes, for privacy and simplicity. No email/password required.
