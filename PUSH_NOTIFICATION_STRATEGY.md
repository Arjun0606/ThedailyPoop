# 🔔 THE ULTIMATE PUSH NOTIFICATION STRATEGY FOR THEDAILYPOOP

**Goal:** Maximize engagement, virality, and MRR by turning every notification into an irresistible hook that pulls users back into the app.

---

## 🎯 THE CORE PRINCIPLE

Every notification must create one of three emotions:
1. **Curiosity** ("What happened?") → Drives opens
2. **FOMO** ("I'm missing out!") → Drives retention
3. **Urgency** ("I need to act NOW!") → Drives monetization

---

## 📱 THE NOTIFICATION INVENTORY

### **TIER 1: CORE ENGAGEMENT (The Hooks)**

#### **1. Ghost Attack Received**
**Trigger:** User gets a ghost attack
**Notification:**
- Title: `👻 Someone just sent a fart your way!`
- Body: `Tap to hear it and guess who's behind it!`
**Why it works:** Simple, direct, creates curiosity without spoiling the game
**Deep Link:** Opens to `GhostAttackReceivedView`

#### **2. Friend Dropped a Poop**
**Trigger:** A friend creates a drop
**Notification:**
- Title: `💩 [Friend Name] just took a dump!`
- Body: `In [City Name] • Tap to see where and react!`
**Why it works:** Creates FOMO (friends are active) + gives context
**Deep Link:** Opens to Map centered on that drop

#### **3. Someone Reacted to Your Drop**
**Trigger:** A friend reacts to your drop
**Notification:**
- Title: `😂 [Friend Name] reacted to your drop!`
- Body: `They sent [emoji] • You earned +5 points!`
**Why it works:** Social validation + shows points connection
**Deep Link:** Opens to your drop details

#### **4. New Poll Created**
**Trigger:** A user in your friend group creates a poll
**Notification:**
- Title: `📊 New poll: "[Poll Question]"`
- Body: `Vote for a friend now • Earn +5 points!`
**Why it works:** Creates urgency (be first to vote) + shows points
**Deep Link:** Opens to Poll tab

#### **5. Poll Results Are In**
**Trigger:** A poll you voted in ends
**Notification:**
- Title: `🏆 Poll results are in!`
- Body: `[Winner Name] won "[Poll Question]" • Tap to see full results!`
**Why it works:** Payoff for participation, creates conversation
**Deep Link:** Opens to Poll results

## 🎨 NOTIFICATION BEST PRACTICES

### **Timing Rules:**
1. **Immediate:** Ghost Attacks, Reactions, Friend Drops (real-time hooks)
2. **Smart Delay:** Leaderboard changes (batch within 1 hour to avoid spam)
3. **Scheduled:** Daily recap (8 PM), Streak reminders (9 AM)

### **Frequency Caps:**
- Max 10 notifications per day per user
- No more than 2 notifications within 30 minutes (except critical actions like attacks)

### **Personalization:**
- Always use friend names
- Always include context (city, emoji, points earned)
- Use dynamic content (leaderboard position, streak count)

### **Actionable:**
- Every notification should deep link to a specific screen
- Clear next action ("Tap to guess", "Vote now", "React")
