## 🔥 The Unique Hook

### **What Competitors Have:**
- YikYak/Fizz/Sidechat = Anonymous posts + reveals
- Locket/BeReal = Daily photo sharing
- Snapchat Maps = Location sharing

### **What We Have (Unique):**
1. **Gossip + Location Verification**
   - Gossip says "@Sarah was with @Jake at the library"
   - Map shows Sarah WAS at the library (with timestamps)
   - Map shows Jake was at the GYM (gossip is fake!)
   - Users become **social detectives**

2. **Screenshot-Proof Reveals**
   - iOS-level DRM protection (black screen on screenshot)
   - Public "Wall of Shame" for screenshot attempts
   - Leaks become **worthless** (no proof possible)

3. **The Trust Deficit Game**
   - Anonymous replies can't be trusted (could be anyone)
   - Only the $1.99 IAP provides **verified truth**
   - Leaks create **more uncertainty** → more IAP sales
