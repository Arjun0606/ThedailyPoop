## 🔥 WHY THIS WORKS

### **Information Gap Theory**
- Show just enough to create curiosity
- Lock the rest to create desire
- Make unlock easy and cheap

### **Loss Aversion**
- "You're SO CLOSE to #2!"
- Creates pain of losing by small margin
- Motivates action

### **Social Validation**
- Leaderboard shows you're popular
- ONE voter proves people care
- Creates need to see all validation

### **Impulse Pricing**
- $0.99 is impulse level
- Less than a coffee
- No time to rationalize

### **Guaranteed Payoff**
- Not gambling, you WILL get answer
- Removes purchase risk
- Makes decision easy
